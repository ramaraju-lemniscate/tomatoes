Blocks
Traffic block — A planned period when no trains run on a section of track, so it's closed for maintenance.
Power block — A planned period when the overhead electric wires (OHE) are switched off, so staff can safely work on or near them.
Power-and-traffic block — Both at once: trains stopped and power off, for work that needs both.
Disconnection — Signalling equipment is taken out of service so it can be maintained. Not the same as a traffic block — it's about the signalling system, not necessarily stopping trains.
Corridor block — A pre-planned window built into the daily timetable for maintenance (e.g. a fixed slot every night). This is timetable policy, not an engineering standard.
Mega block / Jumbo block — A long, consolidated closure, mostly used on Mumbai suburban sections on Sundays, to get major work done in one go.
Other terms
Possession — Having control of a section of track for maintenance work; what a block grants you.
Path — A scheduled slot for a train to run through a section.
Section — A defined stretch of track between two points, the basic unit a block is granted against.
Caution order — An instruction to train crews to run at reduced speed over a stretch of track.
TSR (Temporary Speed Restriction) — A temporary, formally imposed lower speed limit over a stretch of track, usually due to a defect or ongoing work.
GMT (Gross Million Tonnes) — A measure of how much traffic a section carries per year; higher-GMT sections need more frequent maintenance.
DRM (Divisional Railway Manager) — Approves traffic blocks at the divisional level.
Section Controller — Directs train movements and block status in real time from the Control Office.
SSE/P.Way — Senior Section Engineer, Permanent Way — field engineer in charge of track maintenance.
JE/Signal — Junior Engineer, Signalling — field engineer for S&T (signalling & telecom) maintenance.
TPC (Traction Power Controller) — Executes OHE power shutdowns and restorations for power blocks.
The systems
TMS — Track Management System: track defects, inspections, ultrasonic test results, rail fractures.
SMMS — Signalling Maintenance & Management System: S&T assets and schedules.
TDMS — Traction Distribution Management System: overhead wire (OHE) data.
COA — Control Office Application: timetable and live train running.
BDMS — Block & Disconnection Management System: where block requests are raised. It digitised the paperwork, but cross-department planning is still manual — that gap is our problem statement.
